# Design context — Shortlist

## Target audience
In-house technical recruiters and hiring coordinators at software companies.
Not engineers, not hiring managers. They are not technical enough to judge a
resume's claims unaided, which is the whole reason the product exists — the
agent supplies the judgement and the evidence behind it.

They work a queue. On a live req they are looking at 20–200 applicants and are
measured on how many make it to a hiring-manager screen without being sent back.

## Use cases
1. Paste a job description, get a defensible scoring rubric out of it.
2. Drop in a batch of resumes, get every one screened against that same rubric.
3. Read a ranked pipeline and decide who gets a phone screen today.
4. Get a personalised outreach draft for the ones worth contacting.
5. Ask questions about the pipeline in plain language.

## Brand personality / tone
**Editorial dossier.** Warm paper, serif display, hairline rules, dense
small-caps metadata. The reference object is a hiring-committee memo or a
broadsheet obituary column, not a SaaS dashboard.

The reasoning is that this product's output is *an argument about a person,
backed by evidence*. That deserves the visual language of considered written
judgement. A candidate is a dossier entry, not a row in a CRM.

Tone in writing: plain, declarative, unhedged. The agent states its verdict and
shows its evidence. No exclamation marks, no "Great news!", no emoji.

## Non-negotiables
- Every score visible on screen is one click from the evidence behind it. The
  interface must never present a number the recruiter cannot interrogate.
- Risk flags read as questions to ask, never as rejections.
- Nothing in the UI surfaces or invites inference about age, gender, ethnicity,
  nationality, or health.

## Anti-goals
- Cards everywhere. Use rules and whitespace to separate; reserve real surfaces
  for genuinely raised things.
- Dark-mode-with-glowing-accents. This is a light, warm, printed-page product.
- Mono as shorthand for "technical". Scores are set in the serif, at size.
